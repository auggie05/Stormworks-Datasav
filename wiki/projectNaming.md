# How to name projects
Projects use a system similar to the US Department of Defense Joint Electronics Type Designation System.

Example: ANSY/AE-2

The first group is the type of system being created. For anything radio related, ANSY (ANtennaSYstem) is used. For logic/data systems, DASY (DAtaSYstem) is used. For radar or sonar, RASY or SOSY is used respectively. For display systems, DISY is used. The first group will always be XXSY for X system.

The second group followed by a slash is the subtypes. In the example above, AE, stands for Audio, Encrypted. So the above is a encrypted audio antenna system (encrypted radio). 

The third group is a number, which identifies the nth iteration of subtype. ANSY/AE-2 is the second iteration of encrypted audio antenna system.

The fourth and optional group represents updates or parts to the system. Lowercase letters show updates to the system, while uppercase letters show parts of a larger system.

# ANSY Antennasystem
The prefix ANSY shall be used when identifying a radio system.
## First Identifier
The first identifier shall show the type of data being transmitted or received.
A - Audio

D - Data Composite

K - Combined audio, composite, or video

V - Video
## Second Identifier
The second identifier shall show the type of equipment.
### A - Audio
E - Encrypted Radio

R - Radio (One frequency)

T - Radio Telephone (Two frequencies, telephone protocol needed)
### D - Data Composite
E - Encrypted Radio

G - General Data

N - Navigation

R - Remote Control

T - Targeting
### K - Combined audio, composite, or video
A - Automated Air Traffic Control

E - Encrypted Radio

T - Television
### V - Video
G - General Video Share

R - Video used for Remote Control

T - Television
